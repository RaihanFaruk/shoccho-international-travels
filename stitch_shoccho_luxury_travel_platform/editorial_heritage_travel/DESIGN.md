## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined travel atmosphere that synthesizes elite editorial elegance, Islamic architectural balance, and the functional clarity of high-tier transactional travel engines. 

### Visual Ethos
- **Curated Serenity:** Dominated by pristine ivory, warm alabaster, and expansive white space (75%), ensuring high breathing room without visual clutter.
- **Dignified Trust:** Anchored by authoritative deep emerald and forest charcoal to inspire unwavering institutional security for complex visa, Umrah/Hajj, and transatlantic journey planning.
- **Restrained Ornamentation:** Gold accents function strictly as jewelry—highlighting essential micro-moments, seals of authenticity, VIP badges, and delicate rules—never dominating interactive canvases.
- **Architectural Symmetry & Progressive Disclosure:** Clear vertical grid rhythm combined with collapsible complexity to maintain zero cognitive fatigue while retaining comprehensive utility.

### Emotional Target
The user experience evokes the serenity of a bespoke concierge salon, instilling supreme confidence, cultural prestige, and immediate operational clarity.

## Colors

The palette enforces a strict 75-8-8-9 distribution rule:

- **Canvas & Surface Layer (75%):**
  - Base Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Alabaster White (`#FFFDF8`).
  - Warm Card & Module Tint: Ivory Warmth (`#FFF8E7`).
  - Subtle Dividing Surface: Border Tint (`#F4EFE6`).

- **Trust & Authority Accents (8%):**
  - Primary Action / Heritage Anchor: Deep Emerald (`#075E54`).
  - Pressed / Deep Hover Emerald: Midnight Forest (`#043D36`).
  - Muted Emerald Tint: Sage Wash (`#EBF5F3`).

- **Jewelry Gold Highlights (8%):**
  - Focal Gold Accent: Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`).
  - Micro-Border & Filigree Tone: Antique Gold (`#A67C00`).
  - Soft Gold Highlight Surface: Champagne Veil (`#E8D48A`).

- **Photography & Typography Foundation (9%):**
  - Primary Copy & High-Contrast Anchors: Deep Charcoal (`#17201D`).
  - Secondary Editorial Labeling: Muted Slate Charcoal (`#4A5551`).
  - Low-Contrast Wirelines: Ghost Neutral (`#DCD8CE`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere balanced with utility:

- **Editorial Headlines (English):** `Playfair Display` provides high-contrast serif sophistication reminiscent of luxury travel journals. It is utilized exclusively for editorial banners, hero statements, package titles, and milestone metrics.
- **Functional Engine (English):** `Inter` powers flight schedules, booking matrices, multi-step checkout forms, filters, and dense transactional metadata with clean legibility.
- **Bengali Dual Typography Strategy:**
  - Where Bengali language tokens appear in editorial or display settings, map directly to `Noto Serif Bengali` or `Hind Siliguri` (Medium/SemiBold) scaled up by +1px relative to English to equalize x-height balance.
  - Body and UI control labels in Bengali strictly apply `Hind Siliguri` (Regular/Medium) for optical clarity in dense flight and visa forms.
- **Case & Kerning Discipline:**
  - Display serifs use natural sentence casing; never full uppercase.
  - Tracking on labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) is explicitly widened (`+0.06em` to `+0.08em`) when set in Latin uppercase for boarding-pass cues.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px base rhythm arranged inside an architectural, column-structured grid.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px / 1360px max-width container with 32px (`gutter-lg`) gutters and 64px (`margin-lg`) canvas bounds. Employs asymmetric editorial splits (e.g., 5-column editorial context juxtaposed against a 7-column booking widget).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 768px–1024px with 24px (`gutter`) gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 360px–480px fluid container with 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutters and 16px (`margin-sm`) margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Layout Rules
- Sections maintain a minimum of 40px to 80px distance on desktop to prevent visual density.
- Flight matrices, hotel comparisons, and itinerary timelines use strict modular rows with 8px internal padding steps (`space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px) to maximize functional utility without sacrificing readability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses low-contrast architectural micro-borders and soft ambient illumination rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Base Tier (Ground):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and `#FFFDF8` canvases. Zero shadow. Content structured cleanly through 1px hairline borders (`#F4EFE6` or `#E8D48A` with 40% opacity).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Flight Modules):** Elevated on `#FFFFFF` with an ambient, tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 32, 29, 0.04), 0 0 0 1px rgba(166, 124, 0, 0.12)`. The hairline gold border provides structural definition.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Interactive Panels, Booking Overlays):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(7, 94, 84, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #F4EFE6`. Adds depth using a subtle emerald-tinted shadow.
- **Modal / Floating Concierge Tier:** Translucent backdrop with soft blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(23, 32, 29, 0.35)`), combined with cards wrapped in a 1px dual border (outer `#D4AF37` at 30%, inner `#FFFFFF`).

## Shapes

The design language applies subtle, tailored radii (`roundedness: 1` — Soft / 4px base) to project architectural precision, crisp luxury, and institutional authority.

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Inputs, Flight Cards):** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Keeps edges crisp, dignified, and editorial.
- **Contained Modules & Popovers:** 8px (`0.5rem` / `rounded-lg`). Softens larger interactive surfaces without looking playful or juvenile.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Status badges, airline code chips, and category pills utilize full rounded ends (`9999px`) to visually contrast against the razor-sharp grid of itineraries.
- **Architectural Dividers:** Thin horizontal and vertical hairline dividers (1px) with optional geometric diamond or Islamic star terminal accents at intersection points.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Emerald Regal):** Background `#075E54`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`, padding `12px 24px`. On hover, background shifts to `#043D36` with a smooth 150ms transition. Subtle 1px inner border of `#D4AF37` at 20% opacity.
- **Secondary Action (Gold Royal):** Background `#FFFDF8`, text `#A67C00`, 1px border `#D4AF37`. On hover, background `#FFF8E7`.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** Clean typography in `#075E54` with a 1px gold underline (`#D4AF37`) placed 3px below the text baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges (e.g., 'Umrah VIP', 'First Class'):** Background `#FFF8E7`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#A67C00`, radius `9999px`, font `label-sm` with letter spacing `0.08em`.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with `#F4EFE6` border and `#17201D` text. Active: `#075E54` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Datepickers
- **Flight & Hotel Search Field:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px structural border `#E6E2D8`, radius `4px`, text `#17201D`, placeholder `#4A5551`. Focused state: 1px border `#075E54` with a crisp 1px ring `#E8D48A`.
- **Labeling:** Persistent top-aligned micro-label in `label-sm`, uppercase tracking, colored `#4A5551`.

### Cards & Booking Modules
- **Editorial Travel Card:** High-aspect-ratio photography bordered by a 1px gold hairline (`#D4AF37` at 25% opacity), `#FFFFFF` canvas underneath, serif typography title, with a discreet emerald booking pill.
- **Flight Matrix Item:** Horizontal architectural bar segmented into Origin/Duration/Arrival columns. High-contrast typography (`Inter` semi-bold) paired with subtle `#D4AF37` departure-connection lines.

### Lists & Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle with 1px border `#A67C00`. Checked state filled with `#075E54` housing a clean white mark.
- **Flight Itinerary Tree:** Vertical progressive step list connected by a 1px solid `#E8D48A` vector guide, accented by 6px circular emerald nodes.
